## 1. Authority map

### VALIDATED / FROZEN PRODUCT AUTHORITY

From Ensemble Blueprint 0.1 and current application authority:

- Ensemble/Kymaean is a local-first generative theater and creative simulation for persistent characters.
- A Production is a persistent creative object containing world, cast, continuity, and explored history.
- Characters are persistent creative entities, not prompts and not models.
- Character != Performer.
- Creator authority remains primary.
- Production truth, audience-visible information, and character-visible information are distinct projections over one authoritative Production.
- Deterministic access authority precedes relevance/context composition.
- Probabilistic systems may propose; deterministic authority decides.
- Accepted performance is preserved rather than silently rewritten by hidden model committees.
- Consequence must be causal and attributable.
- Local-first means local sovereignty over complete Production state, authority, provenance, and credentials; it does not imply all generation is local.
- Graceful degradation must preserve correctness.
- Evidence outranks enthusiasm and lower validation levels cannot be promoted into higher ones.

### APPROVED BUT NOT YET IMPLEMENTED / VALIDATED AS PRODUCT EXECUTION

Current application authority establishes, among other approved architecture:

- Patch 0012 atomic causal-commit semantics are canonical for implementation but are not yet implementation/runtime authority.
- Accepted Performance and retained Approved consequences are intended to become effective together or neither does.
- Evolved Production -> Access/Context integration remains deferred.
- Final WinUI behavior, Windows AI/NPU execution, packaging, WACK and Store certification are not established.

### STRONG DIRECTION / OPEN DESIGN GUARD

- Keep authority semantics precise; keep creative semantics open.
- Creator-facing dramatic concepts are not automatically engine enums or permanent storage categories.
- UI vocabulary may project authoritative data without freezing one-to-one database types.

### DESIGN PROPOSAL / OPEN

## 2. Recovered product thesis

The strongest recovered formulation is not `prompt -> output` and not `AI characters talking`.

Kymaean evolves state through performance.

A useful internal causal form is:

```text
creator establishes possibility
        ↓
characters encounter bounded circumstance
        ↓
performance expresses agency
        ↓
accepted performance creates consequence
        ↓
consequence changes authoritative history/state
        ↓
changed history alters future possibility
```

The product's durable value is creative continuity: later moments matter because historically specific people, relationships, knowledge, pressures and consequences make them possible.

This is the basis for design synthesis. It is not a public tagline or final navigation model.

## 3. The authoritative creative cycle

Blueprint 0.1 defines seven creative primitives:

```text
CAST -> WORLD -> KNOWLEDGE -> PRESSURE -> SCENE -> PERFORMANCE -> CONSEQUENCE
  ^                                                                  |
  +------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

The design implication is more important than the literal labels:

- **Cast** establishes persistent people and relationships.
- **World** establishes what is true, possible, current or unresolved.
- **Knowledge** establishes asymmetric knowing, believing, suspecting, remembering, claiming and hiding.
- **Pressure** makes the current equilibrium unstable or meaningful.
- **Scene** establishes the bounded present circumstance.
- **Performance** is what a character actually does under that circumstance.
- **Consequence** changes what later situations can mean.

The cycle is recursive. Consequence feeds future Cast, World, Knowledge and Pressure.

Therefore Kymaean's experience architecture should not be reduced to a linear creation funnel.

## 4. Core experience objects and relationships

### Production

A persistent creative reality owned by the creator.

It is not merely a folder of prompts, a transcript, a model session, or a project-management container.

### Character

A historically persistent creative entity whose identity, knowledge, relationships and circumstances can survive performer replacement.

### Performer

A role assignment used to portray a Character. Provider/model identity remains secondary to Character identity.

### Scene

A bounded present circumstance in which specific people, pressures, access conditions and possibilities are relevant.

### Performance / Take

A candidate portrayal becomes authoritative history only through accepted Take semantics and the applicable deterministic authority boundary.

### Consequence

A causally attributable change that matters to future state or future interpretation.

### History

Not merely transcript storage. History preserves causal attribution, accepted performance, consequences, unresolved propositions, changed knowledge/relationships and future hooks.

These concepts form the experience substrate. Their final screen labels and visual manifestations remain design questions.

## 5. Creator relationship model

Blueprint 0.1 preserves four creator postures:

### WATCH

Experience the cast as an ensemble with minimal visible machinery.

### DIRECT

Alter conditions or attention without secretly replacing character agency.

### PERFORM

Temporarily occupy a character's bounded perspective using the same information-authority boundary as an AI Performer.

### WRITE

Use Kymaean as a rehearsal/writers' room: inspect structure, compare takes, branch, revise conditions, examine continuity and create/export in the creator's own workflow.

Critical recovered point:

> These are relationships to one engine, not four products and not necessarily four top-level navigation tabs.

The dominant posture remains intentionally unresolved. Design work must not choose Watch, Direct, Perform or Write as the product's identity merely because one mockup needs a default view.

## 6. Recovered product spaces

Blueprint 0.1 names three conceptual product spaces:

### STUDIO — What could happen?

The creator shapes people, circumstances, relationships, knowledge, pressures, possibilities and authority.

### STAGE — What happens?

The creator experiences current action, social presence, attention and anticipation.

### ARCHIVE — What happened, what changed, and what did it mean?

The creator inspects causal history, accepted takes, consequences, continuity and unresolved movement.

Recovery correction:

These spaces remain valuable **experience functions** and conceptual ancestors. Their precise labels, screen boundaries, layouts and navigation relationships must not be treated as already-final product IA.

The original O0 design lineage is therefore evidence that Kymaean can coherently express creation, performance, bounded perspective and causal history—not evidence that its old dark/gold surface or exact layouts should return.

## 7. Presentation perspective is an orthogonal axis

Production/Creator View, Audience View and Character View are not alternate realities.

They are disclosure projections over one authoritative Production.

```text
Authoritative Production
        ├── Creator / Production projection
        ├── Audience projection
        └── Character-bounded projection
```

Switching perspective changes what may be shown, not what is true.

This means perspective cannot be designed as cosmetic filtering. It carries privacy, dramatic asymmetry and authority meaning.

The exact perspective-switching UX remains open.

## 8. Authority and creative meaning must remain separate

The deterministic engine needs precise distinctions where correctness depends on them:

- truth vs possibility;
- claim vs belief vs memory;
- permitted vs prohibited information;
- proposal vs authoritative commit;
- accepted vs rejected/non-effective history;
- provenance and causal lineage.

The creator-facing experience does not need to expose every internal distinction as a permanent form field or enum.

A surface may communicate, for example, that a relationship is fraying or that someone is holding something back without requiring those phrases to become engine-level types.

Therefore:

> **Product experience ontology is not identical to storage ontology.**

This distinction is a mandatory guard for all subsequent app design.

## 9. The temporal model

Kymaean's experience has at least three meaningful temporal orientations:

### CURRENT POSSIBILITY

What could happen under present conditions.

### PRESENT PERFORMANCE

What is happening now and whose agency is salient.

### CAUSAL HISTORY

What happened, what became authoritative, what changed, and what remains unresolved.

These are connected through consequence, not through generic chronology alone.

R0 TEO evidence strengthens one design insight:

> earlier/later state comparison can materially improve causal observability when the user needs to understand authorship, persistence or changed possibility.

TEO does not mandate animation, split panels, timelines, before/after cards or a website scroll effect.

## 10. R0 visual-law transfer into experience design

The design phase may inherit these semantic resources:

### Human primacy

When people appear, they should be meaningful participants rather than decorative scale figures or generic lifestyle imagery.

### Relationship as structure

Distance, orientation, overlap, grouping, interruption, recurrence and attention can carry relational meaning.

### Identity through invariants

Persistent identity may survive transformed representation when coherent perceptual invariants survive. This allows character identity to be richer than a fixed avatar system.

### Asymmetric evidence

Different perspectives may legitimately expose different evidence from one shared reality. Visuals must not claim private mental states merely because the product has other authoritative data.

### Present structure can carry history

History need not be represented through patina, ruins, nostalgia or explicit flashback.

### Temporal observability

State transition is a justified resource when a causal claim cannot be made legible in one static state.

### Multi-surface burden sharing

No single icon, hero, artwork, screen or animation must explain the entire system.

### Surface quarantine

No R0 renderer surface has production inheritance rights.

## 12. Accessibility and native feasibility as ontology constraints

Accessibility begins at the meaning layer.

No essential distinction may depend solely on:

- color;
- animation;
- spatial position;
- hover;
- fine motor precision;
- visual portrait recognition.

Any relational, identity, authority or state-change representation must have semantic equivalents suitable for keyboard, screen reader, high contrast, scaling, touch and reduced motion.

For the Windows app, the experience model must remain compatible with event-driven WinUI 3 / Windows App SDK implementation and native ARM64 operation. It must not require permanent GPU animation, idle polling, always-on inference or background NPU activity merely to preserve identity or ambience.

Visual richness must be able to become quiescent without losing meaning.

## 14. Recovered experience ontology

The most faithful compact model is:

```text
                     CREATOR AUTHORITY
                           │
                           ▼
                 establishes possibility
                           │
                           ▼
CAST ── WORLD ── KNOWLEDGE ── PRESSURE
                           │
                           ▼
                         SCENE
                           │
                  opportunity / attention
                           │
                           ▼
                     PERFORMANCE
                           │
                    accepted as Take
                           │
                           ▼
                     CONSEQUENCE
                           │
                           ▼
                 AUTHORITATIVE HISTORY
                           │
              changes future conditions
                           │
                           └──────────────► back into Cast / World /
                                            Knowledge / Pressure
```

Across that causal system, the creator may Watch, Direct, Perform or Write.

Across the same authoritative Production, disclosure may be Creator/Production, Audience or Character-bounded.

Studio/Stage/Archive remain useful conceptual functions for possibility, present performance and causal history, but their final product manifestation remains a design problem.

## 15. Smallest unresolved design question after recovery

The next question is **not**:

> Which single experience model is Kymaean?

Nor:

> Which surface should the user see first?

The smallest unresolved question with the highest downstream leverage is:

> **How should the app make the transition between possibility, present performance, and causal history comprehensible without turning them into rigid modes or exposing engineering ontology?**

This is an information/interaction architecture question.

It is small enough to test without selecting an aesthetic and broad enough to prevent premature visual-system work from encoding the wrong product structure.
