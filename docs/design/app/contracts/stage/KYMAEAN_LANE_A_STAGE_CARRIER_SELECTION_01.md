## Decision

The Lane A Stage representation carrier is selected as:

> **Characters as volumes of light on a dark floor, using cone geometry.**

This is explicit repository authority.

## Selected carrier contract

- No human figures, faces, scenery or audience seating are required in the default Stage representation.
- Each present Character may be represented by a cone of light with a floor relationship, restrained Character-associated colour and distinct slow internal texture.
- The creator-facing Character name remains the primary explicit identity channel when shown.
- Stable human-readable/programmatic Character identity is mandatory regardless of colour, image, motion or exact spatial position.
- Colour and internal texture are presentation/data channels only. They do not establish identity, Current Opportunity, knowledge, observation, belief, intent or authority by themselves.
- Representation may depict; authority must establish.
- The Stage remains human/Character-first and quiet. It does not gain permanent roster, marketing copy, generic AI composer, dashboard perimeter or product-thesis narration merely because the carrier is abstract.
- No AI generation is required to keep the carrier alive. Runtime realization should use deterministic gradients/light volumes, texture/noise and text; final WinUI/GPU implementation remains engineering work.
- Stage Performance/motion timing remains unfrozen pending Read-Through evidence.

## Geometry selection

**Cone is selected.**

Reasons:
- inherits the executable O0-C1 `.spotlight-cone` ancestor;
- reads as theatrical illumination rather than containment;
- visibly relates an overhead light source to the Stage floor;
- avoids turning a Character into a portal/chamber/selectable software object.

The cylinder alternative is rejected as the default carrier. Its clearer visible boundedness is useful, but selectability must be solved through semantic hit targets, focus treatment and the floor/name relationship rather than by hardening the Character representation into a container.
