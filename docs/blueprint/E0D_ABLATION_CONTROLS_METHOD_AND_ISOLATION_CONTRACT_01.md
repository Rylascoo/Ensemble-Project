## 6. P01 — relationships omitted

Variant ID: `E0D-RELATIONSHIPS-OMITTED-01`.

The only changed variable is relationship context inclusion. The experimental composition path starts from the same Character-bounded Access output as Full Ensemble and excludes exactly the canonical `MissingRaftContract.RelationshipContextRecordIds` surface from otherwise permitted context. That surface is the derived union of relationship-history records, all six `REL-*` records, and the five relationship-derived memory records.

It must not delete or mutate fixture records, Production truth, fixture version, provenance, Character definitions, Scene state, recent accepted Performance history, current opportunity, or any non-relationship context category. The safe `full-authorized.v1` composer remains unchanged; the omission is a separately labeled experimental composition path.

Named hypothesis: relationship context contributes to relationship-shaped interpretation/behavior and socially intelligible causality rather than merely adding prompt complexity.

Primary mechanism question: **Which transcript more clearly shows relationships shaping interpretation and behavior without numeric or expository relationship narration?**

## 7. P02 — omniscient context

Variant ID: `E0D-OMNISCIENT-CONTEXT-01`.

The only changed variable is Character information asymmetry. The omniscient experimental path is outside `CharacterBoundedAccessControl` and the reference `DeterministicContextComposer`; no `isOmniscient`, debug bypass, special Character ID, or permissive production policy is introduced.

For a source Production state, evaluate the safe reference `CharacterBoundedAccessControl` independently for all three roster Characters, discard the audit `Decisions` surface, and construct a **category-preserving ordinal union** of the three resulting `CharacterAccessProjection`s: SceneState, Pressures, Constitution, Disposition, Circumstance, Observations, Knowledge, Beliefs, Suspicions, Memories, Goals, and Relationships. Deduplicate by canonical RecordId (and relationship RecordId), preserving the reference category and relationship TargetCharacterId. Access decisions, denied Record IDs, authoritative fixture provenance, chronology, credentials, and provider/technical metadata never enter the Character-facing union.

This deliberately removes access-bounded information asymmetry and may expose one Character's otherwise inaccessible knowledge, beliefs, observations, memories, relationship state, or other Character-context records to another. It does **not** authorize Production truth mutation, provenance rewriting, prompt changes, or provider/model changes.

Named hypothesis: Character-bounded information asymmetry contributes to credible agency, causal intelligibility, earned surprise, and dramatic tension.

Primary mechanism question: **Which transcript makes Character-specific knowledge boundaries and asymmetric information feel more causally credible while remaining coherent?**

## 11. Interpretation law

E0-D evidence is descriptive architecture-isolation evidence, not statistical significance. E0-C already demonstrated meaningful stochastic dispersion under the Full reference condition, so a single matched pair must not be inflated into population-level certainty.

After unblinding:

- a Full preference on the preregistered mechanism question is evidence that the named mechanism contributed in that matched observation;
- a tie or ablation preference is evidence that the removed/weakened mechanism did not clearly earn its complexity in that matched observation and must remain live simplification pressure for E0 convergence;
- `INDETERMINATE` or a noncontributing slot is inconclusive, not permission to rerun;
- dimension counts may not be converted post hoc into a winner score;
- E0-D does not replace Run 08 as the selected reference and does not resolve post-E0 ODR-12.

Cross-pair synthesis may describe recurring directional patterns only after all planned pairs reach terminal state. No pair result may change later pair slots, windows, prompts, implementation, rubric, or contribution law.
